# Paseo Plain

Paseo Plain offers an alternative wording of an assistant answer for display. The original answer remains the record of what the assistant said.

## Language

**Original answer**:
The complete assistant answer selected for rewriting, before any wording or structure changes.
_Avoid_: Rewrite input fragment

**Whole-answer rewrite**:
An alternative expression of an original answer that preserves every substantive detail without being bound to its outline or sentence structure.
_Avoid_: Summary, word substitution, copyedit

**Substantive detail**:
A distinct fact, instruction, condition, permission, warning, uncertainty, comparison, or implication carried by the original answer. Repeated emphasis adds no substantive detail.
_Avoid_: Important detail, key point

**Protected content**:
Content whose exact wording and relative order remain fixed in a rewrite, including commands, code, paths, links, quotations, and numbers.
_Avoid_: Editable example

**Voice instructions**:
Saved preferences for how a rewrite sounds, subject to the rules that preserve meaning and protected content.
_Avoid_: Full rewrite prompt
